🔎 QUERY: How do I reset my password?
================================================================================

🤖 ANSWER:
Okay, here’s a detailed resolution suggestion for resetting your PRANIT Portal password, based on the provided documentation:

**Method 1: Resetting with One-Time Password (Recommended)**

This is the most common method, as described in the documents.

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1.  **Access the Portal:** Log in to the PRANIT Portal at [https://etender.powergrid.in](https://etender.powergrid.in).  It’s best viewed in Internet Explorer-11 & above.
2.  **Receive One-Time Password:** Upon your first login to the portal, the system will automatically generate a one-time password and send it to the email address you have on file.
3.  **Change Password:**  At the login screen, use the one-time password to immediately change your password.

**Method 2: Resetting with Security Question/Answer**

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1.  **Access the Portal:** Log in to the PRANIT Portal at [https://etender.powergrid.in](https://etender.powergrid.in).
2.  **Click Reset Password Link:** On the login screen, click on the "Logon Help" option.
3.  **Enter Details:**  On the “Logon Help” screen, you’ll be prompted to enter your Logon ID and mapped E-Mail ID.
4.  **Submit:** Click the "Submit Button" after filling in the required information.
5.  **Answer Security Question:** You’ll be directed to a screen where you’ll need to provide the answer to the security question you set up when personalizing your account (via the "Personalize Link"). *Make sure you enter the correct answer you saved previously*.
6.  **Submit Again:** Click the "Submit Button" after providing the Security Answer.

**Important Notes:**

*   **Security:** The system confirms that the process is secured (refer point no. 8).
*   **Link for Reset Password using security question/answer:** You’ll need your Logon ID and E-Mail.

**If you are still unable to reset your password using these methods, you can contact support by clicking the “Logon Problems? Get Support” option on the portal login screen.**
