🔎 QUERY: How do I reset my password?
================================================================================

🤖 ANSWER:

**Method 1: Resetting with One Time Password**

This is the method recommended for your first login after losing your password.

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1.  **Access the Portal:** Navigate to the PRANIT Portal URL:  https://etender.powergrid.in.  It's best viewed in Internet Explorer-11 & above.
2.  **Initiate Password Reset:** Click on the "Logon Help" option available on the login screen.
3.  **Provide Details:** Enter your Logon ID and mapped E-Mail ID.
4.  **Receive One Time Password:** You will receiveOne Time Password (OTP) on the specified Email ID, which you must use at your first login to change your password.

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

3.  **Provide Details:** Enter your Logon ID and mapped E-Mail ID.
4.  **Answer Security Question:**  Click on the “Reset Password using security question/answer” link. On the next screen, provide the correct answer to the Security Question you set up during personalization. *Ensure you enter the exact answer you saved previously*.
5.  **Submit:** Click the “Submit” button.

**Vendor Registration Process**

If you are a new bidder and not already registered with PowerGrid, you must go through the vendor registration process via the following link: [https://etender.powergrid.in](https://etender.powergrid.in)

**Here’s a breakdown of the registration process:**

**Method 1: New Bidder Registration**

1.  **Access the Portal:** Go to the PRANIT Portal link: [https://etender.powergrid.in](https://etender.powergrid.in)
2.  **Start New Registration:**  You will find a link to “New Bidder? Register Here” – click this to begin the registration process.
3.  **Complete the Form:** You’ll be prompted to fill out the following information:
    *   Organization Name
    *   POWERGRID Allocated 10-digit Vendor code (e.g. 2XXXXXXXXX)
    *   PAN No.
    *   Contact Person Details:
        *   Title
4.  **Submission**: Submit the completed form.

**Important Notes & Prerequisites:**

*   **Timeframe:**  To avoid last-minute issues, ensure your registration is completed and you have a valid user ID and password at least 7 working days before the bid submission deadline.
*   **User ID Creation:** The user ID creation is done either on the Portal or by the RFx owner/co-ordinator on which bidder intends to participate/submit bid response.

**If you are an existing vendor:**

If you have been engaged by PowerGrid earlier for goods/works/services but do not have a Login ID on the PRANIT Portal, contact the RFx owner/co-ordinator for issuance of a Login ID.
